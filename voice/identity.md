# Who is writing

<!-- Fill this in. shortlist.py and draft.py read it on every run. -->

The company's name, what it does and who it serves, in a sentence or two. The
posts go out on the company's LinkedIn page, in first person plural ("we").

## Audience

Who follows the page, and what they already know.

## What the page is building

The reputation you want the company to have on LinkedIn, and what the company
adds to any topic that others don't.

## What the page covers

- Subject one
- Subject two
- Subject three

## Boundaries

- Anything the company never writes about (competitors, pricing, legal
  matters, customers who haven't agreed to be named).
- Anything that needs sign-off before it posts.
