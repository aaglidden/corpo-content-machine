# What's worth a take

How to pick, from a week of news, the handful of items worth an original post.
Read with `identity.md`: that file says what you cover, this one says which
stories within it earn a post.

## Pick it when

- **It's in the company's lane.** One of the subjects in `identity.md`.
- **There's something to say.** A claim to react to, or a surprising move,
  beats a straight announcement.
- **The company reads it differently.** If it has no distinct read, skip.

## Skip it when

- Routine funding, hires and product updates.
- Topics you want to stay away from.
